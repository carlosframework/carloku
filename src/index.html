The whole workflow

One command — or three small motions. No pipelines to babysit, no containers to wrap your app in — you ship the thing itself.

  1. carlos deploy

    One command: it ships your app, promotes it, and waits until the URL serves the new build. When it prints live, it is — seconds, not pipelines.

  2. Want to look first?

    Take it in two motions: carlos ship gives you a private link to the real, running thing; carlos promote makes the exact thing you approved the public thing. Nothing is rebuilt behind your back.

  3. Changed your mind?

    Point at yesterday's version and promote that instead. Going back is the same small motion as going forward.

What you stop thinking about

  • Certificates

    Point your domain at Carloku and the padlock appears. Renewals happen without you.

  • Passwords

    Sign in with a passkey, on the dashboard and in the terminal. There's nothing to invent, reuse, or leak.

  • Idle servers

    Apps doze when nobody's around and wake the instant someone shows up. A quiet app is just quiet.

  • Sending email

    One command and your app has SMTP credentials, on a domain that signs and aligns. No mail service to sign up for, nothing to run.
