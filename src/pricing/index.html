Pricing.

Carloku is priced for convenience and fairness. Four plans covering hobbyists to pro teams.

Choose the plan that works best for you. CARLOS apps scale to zero when they're not in use. You only pay for the time that they're awake.

Members

Members are team members who can access and ship your sites and apps.

Inclusions are per member, per month. They are pooled across the account and do not roll over.

Indexed pricing

Prices are indexed to local affordability, using a World Bank price-level measure for your billing country. There are four bands.

  • Band A pays the full price and gets the full inclusions.
  • Band B pays 75% and gets 75% of the inclusions.
  • Band C pays 50% and gets 50%.
  • Band D pays 35% and gets 35%.

Minimum $1.20 bill based on our own measures. If usage doesn't meet our metered amount, it's not billed for that month.

Carloku is hosting (currently) on AWS. For files served directly from S3, we simply charge the AWS transfer fee.

Half price for anyone

Use the code HALF at checkout for 50% off the subscription, forever. No form, no proof. The inclusions are not halved.

What we promise

Your price is fixed for the year you bought. Rates and country bands are reviewed once a year, in public. Any tightening applies to new subscriptions only. Existing accounts keep the deal they signed up for until they choose to change it.

Tax, paying and cancelling

Prices exclude value-added tax (VAT). VAT is added at checkout according to your billing country and shown before you pay. The merchant is Team Tito Limited, Dublin, Ireland.

You pay by card through Stripe. From your account's Billing page you can change your card, download invoices and cancel. Cancelling keeps the plan running until the end of the year you paid for.

Adding a member to Community or Business charges the rest of the current year for that member straight away, prorated to the day. A member who leaves comes off the bill at renewal.

We keep no card details, billing name or billing address on our side.

Start.

Create an account at console.carloku.com. You sign in with a passkey through Keymail, so there is no password. Every new account starts on Free, and no card is needed. When you want more, open your account's Billing page, choose a plan and pay.
