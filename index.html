The whole workflow

Three small motions. No pipelines to babysit, no containers to wrap your app in — you ship the thing itself.

  1. carlos ship

    Your app goes up, and you get a private link to the real, running thing. Look at it. Poke it. It's live, just not public yet.

  2. carlos promote

    The exact thing you just looked at becomes the public thing. Nothing is rebuilt behind your back; what you approved is what people get.

  3. Changed your mind?

    Point at yesterday's version and promote that instead. Going back is the same small motion as going forward.

What you stop thinking about

  • Certificates

    Point your domain at Carloku and the padlock appears. Renewals happen without you.

  • Passwords

    Sign in with a passkey, on the dashboard and in the terminal. There's nothing to invent, reuse, or leak.

  • Idle servers

    Apps doze when nobody's around and wake the instant someone shows up. A quiet app is just quiet.

Hosted here, never locked here.

The platform under Carloku is open source: CARLOS. You can read every moving part — or run the whole thing yourself, on your own machines, and your apps wouldn't know the difference.

And what you ship is your app as itself — a folder of files, a single binary — not a container wrapped around it.
