🤖 CARLOKU

Ship it. Promote it. Sleep.

Carloku is the hosted CARLOS platform: hosting small enough to hold in your head. You ship, you look at it, you say go. The rest — certificates, passwords, servers idling through the night — stops being your job.

Get started Open the console

The whole workflow

  1. carlos ship. Your app goes up, and you get a private link to the real, running thing. Look at it. Poke it. It's live, just not public yet.
  2. carlos promote. The exact thing you just looked at becomes the public thing. Nothing is rebuilt behind your back; what you approved is what people get.
  3. Changed your mind? Point at yesterday's version and promote that instead. Going back is the same small motion as going forward.

What you stop thinking about

  • Certificates point your domain at Carloku and the padlock appears. Renewals happen without you.
  • Passwords sign in with a passkey, on the console and in the terminal. There's nothing to invent, reuse, or leak.
  • Idle servers apps doze when nobody's around and wake the instant someone shows up. A quiet app is just quiet.
  • What's really running the platform under Carloku is open source. You can read every moving part — or run it yourself. Hosted here, never locked here.
